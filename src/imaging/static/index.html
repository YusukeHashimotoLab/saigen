          Fixed light intensity (for comparison)
          
        

        

          Locks the LED intensity to its initial value (1%) and disables

          White light intensity (white-only mode)
          
        

        

          In white-only mode, every sample is shot at the same light
          intensity. Fix this value when comparing photos side by side or
          comparing absolute transmission. Adjust it if turbid samples come
          out too dark or clear samples blow out. Leave it blank to fall
          back to automatic adjustment.
